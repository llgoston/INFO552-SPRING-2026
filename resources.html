Community Resources

This page provides helpful information for community members who want to live greener, reduce waste, and take part in local environmental efforts.

Recycling Resources

Recycling is one of the easiest ways to reduce waste and protect the environment. Before recycling, make sure items are clean, dry, and accepted by your local recycling program.

  • Recycle paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, aluminum cans, and glass when accepted.
  • Rinse containers before placing them in the recycling bin.
  • Do not recycle food waste, plastic bags, greasy pizza boxes, or broken glass unless accepted locally.
  • Check your city or county website for local recycling rules.

Green Living Tips

Small everyday choices can make a big difference over time. Green living does not have to be expensive or complicated.

  • Use reusable bags, bottles, and containers.
  • Turn off lights and electronics when they are not in use.
  • Walk, carpool, or use public transportation when possible.
  • Buy only what you need to reduce waste.
  • Donate gently used items instead of throwing them away.

Community Clean-Up Ideas

Community clean-ups help neighborhoods look better, feel safer, and bring people together. These events are a great way for residents, students, families, and local organizations to participate.

  • Organize a neighborhood litter pick-up day.
  • Partner with schools, churches, libraries, or community centers.
  • Provide gloves, trash bags, water, and safety instructions.
  • Separate recyclable materials from regular trash when possible.
  • Celebrate volunteers after the event.

Why These Resources Matter

Environmental responsibility starts with awareness. When people know where to recycle, how to reduce waste, and how to get involved, they are more likely to make choices that support a cleaner and healthier community.
