Zero Waste Mixed Herb Stem Salsa Verde

By: Sheri Wetherell

This recipe helps reduce food waste by using herb stems that are often thrown away.

Ingredients

  • 30 g (1 oz) stale bread
  • 100 g (3 ½ oz) mixed herb stems
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 anchovies
  • 40 ml (1 ¾ fl oz / 2 ½ tbsp) olive oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Soak the bread in warm water.
  2. Wash the herb stems and place them in a blender.
  3. Add garlic, vinegar, anchovies, and oil.
  4. Pulse until lightly blended.
  5. Squeeze water from bread and add it to the mixture.
  6. Blend until creamy but not too thick.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Cover with oil and refrigerate for 2 hours.

Yield: 125 ml (4 fl oz / ½ cup)
