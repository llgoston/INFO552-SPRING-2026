Mixed Herb Stem Salsa Verde

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Rest Time: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 30 g stale bread
  • 100 g mixed herb stems (parsley, basil, mint)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 anchovies
  • 40 ml olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Soak bread in warm water.
  2. Wash herb stems and place in blender with garlic, vinegar, anchovies, and oil.
  3. Blend lightly.
  4. Squeeze water from bread and add to mixture.
  5. Blend until creamy.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Cover with oil and refrigerate for 2 hours.
