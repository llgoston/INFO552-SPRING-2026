Zero Waste Mixed Herb Stem Salsa Verde

Sheri Wetherell, June 5, 2019, on Foodista

Did you know about 35-50% of all food produced is wasted? That's just sad. But did you know much of what you toss into your kitchen's compost bin can be transformed into something edible? This recipe is inspired by zero-waste cooking ideas.

About the Cookbook

In their book The Zero Waste Cookbook , authors Giovanna Torrico and Amelia Wasiliev share ways to reduce food waste.

They recommend simple habits like buying local food, planning meals, storing food properly, and using leftovers creatively.

Recipe Description

Salsa Verde is great served with meat, fish, or roasted vegetables. It can also be used as a spread.

Makes 125 ml (4 fl oz / ½ cup)
Prep: 15 minutes
Rest: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 30 g (1 oz) stale bread
  • 100 g (3 ½ oz) mixed soft fresh herb stems
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 anchovies
  • 40 ml (1 ¾ fl oz / 2 ½ tbsp) olive oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Soak the bread in warm water in a bowl.
  2. Wash the herb stems and place them in a blender.
  3. Add garlic, vinegar, anchovies, and oil.
  4. Blend lightly.
  5. Squeeze water from the bread and add it.
  6. Blend again until creamy.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Cover with oil and refrigerate for 2 hours.
  9. Store in the fridge for up to one week.
