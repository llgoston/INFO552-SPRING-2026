Green Team Community Cleanup

Welcome to the Green Team Community Cleanup website. This site encourages community members to take pride in their neighborhoods, protect the environment, and learn simple ways to live greener every day.

Our Purpose

We share green tips, promote cleanup events, and connect people who want to make a positive difference in their community.

Why It Matters

A cleaner community benefits everyone. Picking up litter, recycling properly, and caring for public spaces helps create a safer and healthier environment.

Get Involved

Join a cleanup event, learn sustainable habits, and help keep your community clean and green.
