Zero Waste Mixed Herb Stem Salsa Verde

By Sheri Wetherell

Salsa Verde is great served with meat, fish, or roasted vegetables. You can also serve it on crostini or use it as a sandwich spread.

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Rest Time: 2 hours

Yield: About 1/2 cup

Ingredients

  • 30 g stale bread
  • 100 g mixed herb stems (parsley, basil, mint)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 anchovies
  • 40 ml olive oil (plus extra to cover)
  • Salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Soak the bread in warm water.
  2. Wash herb stems and place in a food processor with garlic, vinegar, anchovies, and oil.
  3. Pulse lightly to blend.
  4. Squeeze water from bread and add it to the mixture.
  5. Blend until creamy but not too thick.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Cover with oil and refrigerate for 2 hours before serving.
