Green Team Community Cleanup

Welcome to the Green Team Community Cleanup website. This site was created to encourage community members to take pride in their neighborhoods, protect the environment, and learn simple ways to live greener every day.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this website is to share helpful green tips, promote cleanup events, and connect people who want to make a positive difference in the community.

Why It Matters

A cleaner community benefits everyone. Picking up litter, recycling properly, reducing waste, and caring for public spaces can help create a safer, healthier, and more welcoming environment.

Get Involved

Whether you want to join a cleanup day, learn better recycling habits, or share helpful resources, this website is a place to start.
