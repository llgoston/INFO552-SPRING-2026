Zero Waste Mixed Herb Stem Salsa Verde

Sheri Wetherell, June 5, 2019, on Foodista

Did you know about 35-50% of all food produced is wasted? That's just sad. But much of what you throw away can actually be used in cooking.

About the Cookbook

The Zero Waste Cookbook by Giovanna Torrico and Amelia Wasiliev shares ways to reduce food waste.

Recipe Description

Salsa Verde is great served with meat, fish, or roasted vegetables. It can also be used as a spread.

Makes 125 ml (4 fl oz / ½ cup)
Prep: 15 minutes
Rest: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 30 g (1 oz) stale bread
  • 100 g (3 ½ oz) mixed herb stems
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 anchovies
  • 40 ml (1 ¾ fl oz / 2 ½ tbsp) olive oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Soak the bread in warm water.
  2. Wash the herb stems and place in a blender.
  3. Add garlic, vinegar, anchovies, and oil.
  4. Blend lightly.
  5. Squeeze water from the bread and add it.
  6. Blend until creamy.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Cover with oil and refrigerate for 2 hours.
  9. Store in the fridge for up to one week.

Source

Recipe from The Zero Waste Cookbook by Giovanna Torrico and Amelia Wasiliev.
