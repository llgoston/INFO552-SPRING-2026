Green Tips

Here are simple ways you can help keep your community clean and protect the environment:

Reduce Waste

Try to use reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Cutting down on single-use plastics helps reduce pollution.

Recycle Properly

Make sure to separate recyclable materials like paper, plastic, and glass. Follow your local recycling guidelines.

Pick Up Litter

If you see trash on the ground, pick it up safely and dispose of it properly. Small actions make a big difference.

Save Energy

Turn off lights when not in use and unplug devices to conserve energy.

Protect Green Spaces

Respect parks, trails, and public areas. Keeping these spaces clean helps everyone enjoy them.
