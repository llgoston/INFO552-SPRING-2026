Green Tips

Simple ways to help protect the environment

Reduce Waste

Use reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Cutting down on single-use plastics helps reduce pollution.

Recycle Properly

Separate recyclable materials like paper, plastic, and glass. Follow your local recycling guidelines.

Pick Up Litter

If you see trash on the ground, pick it up safely and dispose of it properly. Small actions make a big difference.

Save Energy

Turn off lights when not in use and unplug devices to conserve energy.

Protect Green Spaces

Respect parks, trails, and public areas. Keeping these spaces clean helps everyone enjoy them.
